Being a professional developer, I've been working with Pull Requests for years. 

For years my tool of choice was [Trailer.app](https://ptsochantaris.github.io/trailer/). It's minimal UI and it's customizability were the main reasons it was my preferred solution for years. However, this level of customizability also came with a great deal of complexity. I often failed to make PRs appear, despite having configured the levels of notifications I wanted to receive. Also, why should I have to select between v3 and v4 of the Github API? Finally, I always found it difficult to understand the difference between "Participating" and "Mine". I am sure that there is good reason for all of this. But all I wanted was the list of PRs that were waiting for my review, and the list of PRs I opened.

That's why I (and Claude) created [PRty time](https://github.com/fragkakis/prty-time). I heavily borrowed by beloved Trailer.app's layout, applied my sensible defaults, without introducing it's complexity. It's not nearly as powerful as Trailer.app, but it's perfect for me. I've also registered it with Homebrew for people to try it out easily.

Happy coding!
